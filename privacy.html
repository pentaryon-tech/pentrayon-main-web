Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 13, 2026 | Last Updated: October 13, 2026

1. Information We Collect

Pentaryon Technology collects information you provide directly to us when contacting our engineering team, requesting hardware evaluations, or subscribing to company updates. This includes your name, email address, company affiliation, and project inquiries.

2. Device & Technical Telemetry Data

For connected developer kits, embedded evaluation boards, and cloud dashboards, we may collect technical telemetry (such as device IDs, firmware versions, uptime, and system performance logs) strictly used for quality assurance, bug tracking, and real-time synchronization diagnostics.

3. How We Use Information

We use collected data solely to deliver, maintain, and optimize our hardware and software solutions, respond to customer inquiries, and ensure the security of our distributed systems. We never sell personal information to third parties.

4. Cookies and Web Analytics

Our website uses minimal, privacy-preserving technical cookies strictly necessary for site navigation and performance. We do not employ intrusive cross-site tracking cookies.

5. Data Security & Storage

We implement industry-standard cryptographic encryption (TLS/HTTPS, AES-256 for firmware payloads) and secure access controls to protect your data against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

6. Privacy Inquiries

If you have any questions or requests regarding your personal data under global data protection laws (such as GDPR or India's DPDP Act), contact our Data Protection Officer:
