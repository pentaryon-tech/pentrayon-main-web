AERO One

Hands-Free Following, Smart Sentry Monitoring & Aerial Companion Robotics

Meet AERO One: the next generation of autonomous personal aerial robotics. Designed from the ground up for hands-free following, smart sentry monitoring, and aerial companion robotics, AERO One bridges the gap between commercial camera drones and intelligent edge computing.

Unlike traditional drones that rely solely on bulky GPS tracking beacons or smartphone tethering, AERO One uses true computer vision. Its onboard neural engine perceives faces, measures biometric facial embeddings in 128-dimensional space, and dynamically maneuvers its quad-rotor propulsion system to keep you locked at the center of the frame.

Whether you're recording action videos hands-free, testing autonomous flight algorithms, or securing your premises with smart intruder identification, AERO One is engineered to deliver unmatched precision, safety, and autonomy.
